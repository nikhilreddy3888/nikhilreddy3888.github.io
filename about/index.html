I'm an Applied Research Scientist at ServiceNow, working at the intersection of multimodal AI, document intelligence, retrieval, evaluation, and AI safety.

The short story

I build systems that help people work with information across documents, languages, images, audio, and video. My research explores how these systems understand content, where they fail, and how to make them more dependable in real use.

At ServiceNow, I have worked on an agent for summarization, question answering, extraction, classification, and cross-document reasoning. I also helped build multilingual and multimodal search across more than ten languages and more than twenty document types.

My background in software engineering shapes how I approach research: a result matters most when it can become a usable, measurable system.
